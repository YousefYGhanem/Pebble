Pip grows up: one box for everything, in your own words, a free AI out of the box, and natural voices in 50+ languages.

## Install

Download **Pebble-Setup-0.5.0.exe** below and run it, or let Pebble 0.4.x update itself (it checks a minute after it starts). The installer is still not code-signed, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time: **More info → Run anyway**. Some antivirus programs may also flag it; see [If an antivirus flags Pebble](https://github.com/YousefYGhanem/Pebble#if-an-antivirus-flags-pebble).

## Pip's page: one box for everything

**Ctrl+Alt+K** opens Pip's page (the Chat page, now called Pip) with the box ready. Type in plain words, in English or Arabic:

- **Instant answers** show above the box as you type, and Enter takes them (copies the answer, starts the timer, picks the colour):
  - sums: "what's 15% of 80", "12 times 8.5"
  - units and currencies: "how many miles is 5 km", "how much is 100 dollars in euros" (the European Central Bank's daily rates)
  - the time anywhere: "what time is it in Tokyo", "9am London in New York"
  - translations: "translate good morning to French", "ترجم صباح الخير إلى الفرنسية"
  - timers: "set a timer for 10 minutes for tea", "remind me in 5 min to stretch": the island counts it down
  - colours: "#53e4ff", "rgb(…)", and "pick" reads the colour under the mouse
  - tools for what you copied: "format the json", "base64", "camelCase", "sha256", "count", a Unix time as a date
- **Anything else** goes to Pip as a message. Tab asks Pip even when there is an instant answer. "explain this" and "summarize this" send what you copied to Pip, only when you press Enter.
- **Settings → Pip's chat → Instant answers:** the language translations go into, the clipboard tools, and explain/summarize.

## A free AI out of the box

A new Pebble answers right away with a free AI (Pollinations, an open model; no account or key). Your own Claude Code, Codex or an API key is one click away in Settings → Pip's chat, and the note there says plainly where messages go. If you already used Pebble, nothing changes: you keep Claude Code.

## Natural voices

The speaker on an answer (and *Read answers aloud*) now reads it **in its own language** with a natural voice that runs on your PC (Piper, 50+ languages). A language's voice downloads once, about 60 MB, the first time an answer in it is read; nothing you read is sent anywhere. Settings → Pip's chat → Voice switches back to Windows' voices.

## Connectors

- **GitHub:** your pull requests' CI live on the island while it runs (checks done of all), then passed or failed.
- **Local servers (new):** which of your dev servers answer on this PC (3000, 5173, 8080…), one click to open, a notification when one stops.
- **Countdowns (new):** days until the dates you care about, the nearest as a chip.
- **Watch a value:** price alerts, only when the value goes above or below your limits.
