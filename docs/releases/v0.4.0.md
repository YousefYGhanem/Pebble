A big one: Pebble picks up the best ideas from other notch apps, adds a calmer day around your agents (reminders, prayer times, screen time), and got a full review for speed, safety and polish.

## Install

Download **Pebble-Setup-0.4.0.exe** below and run it, or let Pebble 0.3.x update itself (it checks a minute after it starts). The installer is still not code-signed, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time: **More info → Run anyway**.

## Your agents

- **Claude's plan usage, also for the Claude app:** session, week and model limits come straight from Anthropic with your Claude sign-in, so they show even when no status line runs. An expired sign-in is renewed for you.
- **Warnings before the limit:** at 75%, 90% and 100% of a session or week, once each, with a ring on the island. The Agents page has a **Usage** tab with every limit and when it resets.
- Claude's suggested next step shows on the island, and a click answers it.

## The island

- **Page tabs on top**, a glass edge, four theme presets, and an **Auto** theme that follows Windows' light or dark mode.
- **Live-Activity peeks:** short notices when something changes (sound device, Caps Lock, keyboard language, microphone, battery, usage).
- **Where along the top:** left, middle or right (Settings → Island).
- **Minimal island** and a **game strip** with your PC's readings during a game.
- Reminders, notices and a meeting's **Join** stay put when you point at them, so their buttons are easy to hit.
- In Arabic, the open pages run right to left.
- Empty pages say what goes there and offer the one thing to do.

## Your day

- **Reminders** (Settings → Reminders): water and stand-up, within the hours you choose. Time away counts as a break.
- **Prayer times**, worked out on this PC from your city: the next one on the island and on Home, a reminder at each (it can pause your music).
- **Screen time:** your top apps today on Home, counted on this PC only.
- **Notes & To-do:** tasks with a time get a reminder on the island (Done / In 10 min), and can repeat daily.
- **Calendar Join:** two minutes before an online meeting (Teams, Zoom, Meet, Webex) the island shows a Join button.
- Your city is asked once, for prayer times and the Weather connector.

## Music, files and clipboard

- **Player | Lyrics**, a wavy progress bar, and a lyrics column that follows the song.
- **Apps:** each app's own volume and mute, like Windows' volume mixer (Music page).
- **Focus sounds:** rain, deep or waves while a focus round runs.
- **Snip** (Files page): snip part of the screen; it lands on the shelf and its text goes to the clipboard list.
- **Clipboard pictures:** copied images join the clipboard list.
- **Quick controls on Home:** volume, mute, microphone and power mode.

## Faster and lighter

- While an answer streams into Pip's chat, only the chat is sent to the island.
- More of Pebble rests while you are away, in a game or tucked away, and it wakes within seconds of your return.
- Fewer writes to disk and fewer messages between Pebble's parts.

## Safer

- **Phone drop** listens only on your Wi-Fi or cable address, caps uploads per session, and marks received files as from the internet, so Windows asks before running one.
- **Pip's chat through Claude** can no longer run commands, edit files or use the web, whatever your Claude Code allows.
- No Pebble window can navigate away or open another window; links open in your browser.

## Fixes

- After sleep: prayer reminders do not fire late, screen time does not count the night, and reminders do not fire the moment you return.
- Renewing the Claude sign-in can no longer lose the new tokens.
- Daily to-dos keep their time across a daylight-saving change.
- Times use one kind of digit in Arabic; CPU, RAM and agent states are translated.
- The island no longer becomes click-through after a few clicks.
