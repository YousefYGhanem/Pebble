Lighter for an always-on app, a day summary, usage alerts, and Pebble made more yours.

## Install

Download **Pebble-Setup-0.2.0.exe** below and run it, or let Pebble 0.1.x update itself (it checks a minute after it starts). The installer is still not code-signed, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time: **More info → Run anyway**.

## Lighter on your PC

Pebble runs all day, so it should barely register. Measured with an agent working:

| | 0.1.1 | 0.2.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPU (one core) | ~39% | ~6% |
| Private memory | ~593 MB | ~346 MB |
| Tucked away | – | ~0.1% |

- The island's pulsing "working" dot repainted the whole see-through window at the screen's full refresh rate; it now pulses in steps (the biggest saving, in the graphics process).
- The island draws at most 60 frames a second, also on 144/240 Hz screens.
- Built-in connectors share one process instead of one each (~70 MB per connector saved). A connector you add still runs in its own.
- Fewer updates sent to the island (grouped, and skipped when nothing changed), a cheaper clipboard check, quieter drag detection in the native helper, and Pip's chirps release the sound device afterwards.

## New

- **Tools rest when nobody needs them** (Settings → General → *Rest when*): while you are away or a game runs full screen, the clipboard, volume, sound wave, PC readings, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth check and connectors pause; on battery, connectors and PC readings. Connectors refresh once when Pebble wakes.
- **Tucked away, everything rests**: right-click the island (or Ctrl+Alt+H) and every service stops, music too. Agents still reach Pebble, and a question brings the island back.
- **Usage alerts**: a notification and a worried Pip when Claude Code or Codex passes **80%** and **95%** of its session or weekly limit, with the time it resets (Settings → General → Notifications).
- **Today with your agents** on the Home page: tasks done, time worked, files changed, and how much of each CLI's week (or tokens) went today. Yesterday's shows until today has something.
- **Island colours** (Settings → Look & Pip): each colour and the corner roundness, with a live preview. A change saves your own theme (Custom), starting from the one you use.
- **Pip's look** (Settings → Look & Pip): his colours in the island: the theme's, lavender, cream or graphite (like the app icons), or your own body and visor colours.
- **A new icon**: Pip as a river pebble, for the app, the installer and the tray. Settings → Look & Pip → *App icon* picks lavender, cream or graphite for the tray and Pebble's windows.
- **Welcome tour**: a new step shows how to open, close and tuck away the island, as a small looping demo.
- Right-click to tuck is now explained: a tooltip on the island, a hint next to the tuck shortcut, and the tray item.

## Fixed

- The Music page's volume slider really follows Windows' volume now. The native helper could lose a request when several arrived at once at startup (the volume watch was one of them).
- Settings → Look & Pip: the icon previews load.
- A copy of Pebble built locally (`npm run app`) says it does not update itself, instead of showing an error.

## For developers

- `npm run app` builds and starts the real `Pebble.exe` (dist/win-unpacked) instead of `electron.exe`.
- Connectors: `pebble.every` jobs pause while Pebble rests and run once when it wakes (docs/PLUGINS.md).

## Experimental: chat apps

`pebble-chat-0.1.1.zip` (unchanged) is the browser extension for claude.ai, ChatGPT, Gemini and DeepSeek. It and the desktop chat-app beta read those sites and type for you, which their terms of service may not allow (automated access): using them may put your chat account at risk. Both are off until you set them up.
