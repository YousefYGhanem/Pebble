A usability release: Pebble is easier to read, safer to answer, works from the keyboard, and explains itself where you use it.

## Install

Download **Pebble-Setup-0.3.0.exe** below and run it, or let Pebble 0.2.x update itself (it checks a minute after it starts). The installer is still not code-signed, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time: **More info → Run anyway**.

## Answering agents

- **Safer answers:** the ask card ignores clicks in its first 0.6 s, since it grows right under the mouse at the top of the screen. For tools that run commands or change files (Bash, Write, Edit…), Allow is no longer the highlighted answer. A question highlights an option only when the agent itself marks it "(Recommended)".
- **Claude's suggested next step shows on the island:** when a turn ends with a question (the Claude app's next-step box), the small island shows it and the Agents page shows its answers as buttons. A click replies.
- The ask card says which agent asks and how many wait ("Claude Code · 1 of 3 waiting").
- **In a full-screen game** a question appears only as the thin line. Point at it to answer, and it hides again afterwards.
- Messages from helper agents and background tasks no longer replace your prompt on the island.

## Status you can read

- Status is a shape as well as a colour: ready ○, working ●, done ✓, stopped !, needs you ?.
- Tucked away, the line is dashed when an agent stopped and dotted when one needs you.
- Hover-open waits until the pointer slows down, so the island no longer opens as you pass it on the way to a tab.
- Tucked away, clicks go through to the window below. Pointing at the line still brings it back.

## Keyboard and screen readers

- **Ctrl+Alt+P** gives the island the keyboard: Tab and Enter answer, **Esc** closes it and returns you to your app.
- The page row is one Tab stop: arrows, Home and End.
- The music position bar works with the keyboard and is easier to hit with the mouse.
- Screen readers hear what finished, stopped or needs you.
- The Settings choices and the welcome tour expose their state, and the tour's demo can be paused.

## Pages

- The page row has three groups. Notes has its own icon. **Settings → Island → Pages** leaves out pages you don't use.
- **Undo for 5 s** after:
  - clearing the clipboard or the shelf
  - removing a file
  - deleting a note
  - clearing history in Settings
- **Clipboard:**
  - Remove or **pin** single entries. Pinned ones stay at the top and survive Clear.
  - Keys and tokens show only their ends.
- **Files:** one click opens a file, the tools stay visible, and files without a picture show their type (PDF, TXT).
- **Focus:** when a round ends, "Another round" or "5 min break", and the rounds done today. Stop is the quieter button.
- **Notes:** Delete moved to the header (it covered the text), and each note shows when it changed.
- **Widgets:** cards that open a link show an arrow, and a connector's problem has **Fix in Settings**.
- **Chat:** Stop shows a stop square, and the empty chat offers starters ("Look at a window", "Explain an error", voice).
- **Home:** the fourth tile is always Files.
- **Agents:** "Hide" is now "Hide from island" (the agent keeps running).

## Settings

- **Search** (Ctrl+F) finds any setting by its name or hint, opens its section and highlights it.
- **Look & Pip** is now two tabs, **Appearance** and **Pip**:
  - The colours sit behind "Customize colours".
  - While your own theme is on, there's a way back to the default.
- Connectors show On, Off or Needs attention, and show their fields only when on.
- Keys say "✓ Saved, encrypted" or "Not set", and a saved chat key can be removed.
- Shortcuts read Ctrl+Alt+P, have a reset, and warn when another app holds them.
- Island sizes and the experimental chat apps are folded away.
- Restart and Quit sit apart, and "Restart now" appears after a port change.
- **Notifications by source:** needs you, done, connectors, focus/phone/outfits.
- Problems are described in plain words, with where to fix them.

## Look

- One shared style for the island, Settings and the tour: the same layers, strokes, primary button and corner radii, all following the theme.
- Links meet text contrast (about 6:1).
- Windows contrast themes are supported in Settings and the tour.
- Living skins show only on the resting island, and the vines are sage, so they can't be mistaken for the green "done".
- In Arabic, each line in the island aligns to its own start.

## Tray and tour

- **Tray:**
  - The tooltip says what's going on ("Pebble · 2 working · 1 needs you").
  - "Tucked away" is a checkmark.
  - Notifications and Sounds switch from the menu.
  - An available update comes first.
- **Welcome tour:**
  - You try opening, closing and tucking the real island, and each step gets a tick.
  - Pip's name comes at the end.
  - The terminals step names each shell's file.
  - With no AI found, it lists the ones Pebble follows.
- **Tips** show once, the moment they help: the first question, the first open island, the first shelf file. Settings → General shows them again.

## Fixed

- **Focus guard:** the breathing screen now appears only while a focus timer runs (it fired all day before). A switch keeps the old "always".
- **Security findings:**
  - Watch a value decodes `&amp;` last.
  - The RSS connector strips tags fully.
  - The CI token is read-only.

## Experimental: chat apps

`pebble-chat-0.1.1.zip` (unchanged) is the browser extension for claude.ai, ChatGPT, Gemini and DeepSeek. It and the desktop chat-app beta read those sites and type for you, which their terms of service may not allow (automated access): using them may put your chat account at risk. Both are off until you set them up.
