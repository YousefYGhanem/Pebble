Your accessories' batteries on the island, a clearer song line, help for the phone drop, and Pebble on the Microsoft Store.

## Install

Download **Pebble-Setup-0.5.2.exe** below and run it, or let Pebble 0.5.x update itself (it checks a minute after it starts). Or get it from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9PJNR7CT4ZN7) (as Pebble Pet), which keeps it up to date. The installer from GitHub is still not code-signed, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time: **More info → Run anyway**.

## Accessory batteries

- **On Home:** a row with your wireless mouse, keyboard, headphones and controllers, each with its battery, lowest first. A ⚡ shows the ones charging.
- **When one connects:** a peek with its type, name and battery, for Bluetooth headphones, a game controller and more.
- **When one runs low:** a peek at 20%, and a Windows notification at 10%. Each is told once, until it charges again.
- **What it reads:**
  - Bluetooth devices of any brand that report their battery to Windows (the same number as Windows' Settings);
  - Xbox and other game controllers;
  - Razer wireless mice and keyboards through their receiver;
  - Logitech devices on a Unifying, Bolt or Lightspeed receiver.
- **Settings → Notifications → Accessory batteries** turns it off.

## Music

The song's line now shows the whole song: a faded wave in the album's colour under the part already played, waving with it while the song plays. The played part also shows again while a song is paused (it had vanished).

## Phone drop

The Files page has **Not working? Steps to try** while the phone drop is on:
- the same Wi-Fi;
- VPN and private DNS;
- Chrome's *Nearby devices* permission on Android, *Local Network* on iPhone;
- another firewall on the PC;
- routers that keep devices apart.

## Microsoft Store

Pebble is on the Microsoft Store as **Pebble Pet**, and every release goes there too. The Store version keeps its data in `%USERPROFILE%\.pebble`, so your coding tools can reach it.
